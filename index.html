AI use cases for marine construction

On site, measuring is largely solved. The step from measurement to a decision somebody signs is not. BBA closes that step. Eight AI use cases on one BBA operations screen, each ending in an action and a record rather than a picture, and the same record keeps running the facility after handover.

The eight AI use cases

USE CASE 01

As built register

Covered work cannot be proved later. Captured once while still open, then sealed.

Inputs
site capture, drone surface, inspection form
Action
sealed entry against the work item
Evidence
BBA Nexus, hash chained ledger in production

USE CASE 02

Water pollution

Turbidity and sediment plumes from dredging and filling, watched against the silt curtain line. Foam, discolouration and oil sheen as well.

Inputs
fixed cameras, drone passes
Action
time stamped notice when a plume crosses the curtain, sealed in the record
Evidence
BBA water quality module, in production, built for coastal water

USE CASE 03

Waste and debris

Debris on the works, access roads and the waterline.

Inputs
fixed cameras, drone passes
Action
cleaning order per zone, closed with a frame
Evidence
measured on BBA reference imagery, precision 0.885. Calibrated on this site in week one

USE CASE 04

Vehicles and plant

Movements counted, plant identified: trucks, excavators, mixers, cranes, loaders.

Inputs
gate and haul road cameras, drone passes
Action
trip count per shift, feeding the quantity check
Evidence
BBA vehicle module, measured. Open vocabulary verified on plant classes

USE CASE 05

Work progress

Progress per zone against what was declared.

Inputs
drone orthophoto, fixed cameras, delivery notes
Action
progress updated, flagged where claim and imagery differ
Evidence
BBA change detection module with a progress mode, in production

USE CASE 06

Safety and exclusion zones

Helmet and vest compliance, people entering live lifting zones, and craft entering the marine exclusion zone.

Inputs
fixed cameras, vessel position feed, zones per shift
Action
incident file opened, with who was notified and when
Evidence
BBA open vocabulary detection, verified on helmet, vest and plant classes

USE CASE 07

Shoreline and fill change

Shoreline and fill areas, including accretion and erosion on the beaches either side. Satellite for the wide view, drone for the surface.

Inputs
satellite imagery and elevation, drone photogrammetry
Action
change report per period, two sources compared
Evidence
BBA satellite and elevation pipeline, built and running

USE CASE 08

Night GNSS risk advisory

Precise dredging, placement and drone mapping all rest on satellite positioning, which degrades at certain hours in this region.

Inputs
BBA GNSS radar, operating area and planned window
Action
advisory before the shift, so precise work is not scheduled into a bad window
Evidence
BBA GNSS radar in operation, demo available on request

How it is put together

A multi input system. No source is trusted on its own. Anything carrying money or responsibility is read by at least two, and the two are compared before anything is written.

The aircraft is a foundation

The drone is not an accessory to this system. It is one of the two things holding it up.

Fixed cameras see what they are pointed at. The aircraft reaches the breakwater head, the fill face, the water beside the works. It is what makes a second independent reading possible at all.

Aerosmart runs the flight side, BBA builds the decision side on top of it. Both halves carry load.

From construction into operation

Nothing is rebuilt at handover. The register written during construction is the register the facility is run from.

LEVEL 1

Manual

BBA watches, sorts and records. Every action is taken by your team. The record accumulates from day one.

LEVEL 2

Assisted

BBA proposes the action and the evidence behind it. A person approves. Approvals and rejections are both kept.

LEVEL 3

Automatic, with a gate

BBA acts inside the rules you set and stops at the gate. Anything outside the rules, or where sources disagree, still goes to a person.

Set per use case and per zone, raised when the record shows it has earned it. The facility can run almost entirely on BBA while every exception still reaches a named person.

Why this is BBA

  • BBA writes the software. Not a reseller layer on somebody else's platform. The vision engine, the decision ledger and the operations screen are ours, and BBA stays visible on every one of them.
  • BBA Nexus keeps a record that can be checked. Hash chained and independently verifiable, so an entry made months ago can still be proved to be the entry that was made.
  • BBA sets autonomy per use case. Manual, assisted, or automatic inside a gate. The owner decides how much the system does, and changes it whenever they want.
  • BBA runs mixed sources on one screen. Fixed cameras, drone and satellite together, cross checked against each other rather than trusted one at a time.
  • BBA stays after handover. The same company and the same record, from the first excavation through years of operation.
